Our Story

A Khammam district favourite in Naidupeta

Dheeran has been serving Naidupeta since 2012 — a casual dining spot known for its vibrant, cozy ambience and fast, friendly service, well known to locals in the area.

The kitchen runs Andhra, Chinese, Biryani, and North Indian menus side by side, with both AC and open-air seating so families, groups, and quick solo diners all have a spot.
